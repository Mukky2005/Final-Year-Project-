Company ABC - Corporate Credit Card & Procurement Audit Standards 2026
Document Owner: Corporate Finance & Treasury
Target Audience: Finance Employees & Accounting Staff

1. Purchasing Thresholds
Corporate credit cards are issued to department leads with a default single-transaction limit of $2,500. Any purchase exceeding $2,500 requires pre-authorization through the NetSuite procurement module with approval from the Vice President of Finance.

2. Software Subscription Audits
All SaaS recurring software subscriptions must undergo quarterly usage reviews. Licenses that have remained inactive for greater than 45 days will be automatically flagged for decommissioning during the month-end accounting close.

3. Tax and VAT Compliance
Itemized VAT and sales tax invoices must be attached to every expense report. Missing tax documentation will result in a 14-day hold on subsequent reimbursement disbursements.
