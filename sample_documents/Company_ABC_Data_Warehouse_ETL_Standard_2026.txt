Company ABC - Analytics Datamart & ETL Operational Standards 2026
Document Owner: Enterprise Data Strategy Group
Target Audience: Business Analysts & Data Stewards

1. Metric Standardization (SSOT)
All cross-functional business metrics including Net Recurring Revenue (NRR), Monthly Active Users (MAU), and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) must be sourced strictly from the certified Gold-tier Snowflake datamarts. Ad-hoc calculations in client spreadsheets are prohibited for board reporting.

2. Data Freshness SLA
Financial analytics models must be refreshed daily by 05:00 UTC. Operational marketing campaign datamarts must be refreshed hourly during core business operations.

3. Schema Drift Protocols
Any schema changes affecting upstream ingestion tables require a minimum 7-day notification window submitted via Jira to the Data Governance Board.
